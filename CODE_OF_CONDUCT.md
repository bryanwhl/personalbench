# Code of Conduct

Personalbench welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help people understand the project, keep criticism about the work, and respect others' time and privacy.

Expected behavior includes being considerate, explaining disagreements with evidence, accepting corrections, and giving credit. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when needed to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate and consider the impact on affected people.

Use GitHub's abuse-reporting controls for harassment or private-information exposure. For project moderation questions, contact a repository maintainer through an available private channel; do not include private details in a public issue. Maintainers will respect confidentiality as far as possible and will not retaliate against a good-faith report.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces maintained for this project.
